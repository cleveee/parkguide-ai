# Ethics, reliability, and AI-use evidence

These are implementation reports. Gold verification, semantic grading, and the final held-out benchmark remain subject to real human review. Diagnostic scores are not assignment-valid gold accuracy.

Coded safeguards: retrieved text is explicitly untrusted; generation is source-only; citation/number/quote checks can withhold output; current park safety cannot be inferred from dated text; high-stakes questions receive limitations and referral. UI states the saved-snapshot limit. Robots/allowlists/rate control and directory exclusions constrain crawling. Pattern-based contact redaction and PDF/table extraction need review.

AI assistance: Codex and scoped subagents implemented ingestion, chunking/storage/retrieval, generation, evaluation, and reports. Candidate questions and diagnostic grades are AI proposals. No agent impersonates a human verifier. Every metric must be explained from formulas and saved outputs. GitHub protection/team PRs, individual contributions, and a classroom demonstration remain deferred course requirements.

Paid synthetic retrieved-injection and expired-source diagnostics are retained in artifacts/latest-safeguards.json. These fixtures are not NPS corpus records or formal benchmark items.
