          Restaurants and Groceries

             

            South Rim Restaurants and Groceries (Inside the Park)

            Due to the 2026 Bright Angel Creek Flash Flood and Stage 4 Water Restrictions, many restaurants are closed or have shortened hours until further notice. For current closures and hours, please click here.

            Throughout the year, Free Shuttle Buses provide transport between the Visitor Center, campgrounds, canyon views, lodges, restaurants and gift shops. Park once and let the shuttles take you around the village.

            Grand Canyon Visitor Center

            • Canyon Adventures Bike Tours and Café - Located at the plaza between the Visitor Center and the Bookstore. Offers a coffee bar, "grab-and-go" Breakfast and Lunch, and hiker/biker supplies. Open year-round. Phone 928-638-3055.

            Market Plaza

            (the business center of the village with a post office, ATM machine, campground and RV campground.)

            Canyon Village Market (open daily, year-round)

            • Canyon Village Market "General Store" - Located west of the parking lot. Offers groceries. Phone: 928-638-2262
            • Deli at the Marketplace - Located in the General Store. Currently CLOSED.

            Mather Campground - Camper Services Building

            • Camper Services Laundromat and pay showers are open daily. Camper Store is open during the summer. Camper Services is located at the entrance to Mather Campground. Currently CLOSED.

            Yavapai Lodge (open daily, year-round)

            • Yavapai Dining Hall - Located north of the parking lot. Currently CLOSED.
            • Yavapai Café - Located in the same building as the Yavapai Lodge Restaurant. Offers coffee and snacks.
            • Yavapai Lodge Tavern - Located in the same building as the Yavapai Dining Hall. Offers Lunch and Dinner.

            Grand Canyon Village Historic District

            El Tovar Hotel (open daily, year-round)

            • El Tovar Dining Room - Located on the rim. Offers Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner. Dinner reservations recommended. Phone 928-638-2631.
            • El Tovar Lounge - Located inside the El Tovar hotel, with a side entrance facing the rim. Offers drinks and light meals.

            At Bright Angel Lodge (open daily, year-round)

            • Arizona Steakhouse - Located on the east end of Bright Angel Lodge. Access is from outside the lodge.
            • Fred Harvey Burger - Located inside Bright Angel Lodge.
            • Bright Angel Fountain Located on the canyon-side patio of Bright Angel Lodge. Offers coffee, tea and light meals in the morning, and ice cream, beverages and grab-n-go sandwiches in the afternoon.
            • Bright Angel Tavern - Located inside Bright Angel Lodge. Offers drinks and light meals in the evening.

            At Maswik Lodge

            • Maswik Food Court and Pizza Pub: Located within Maswik Lodge at the southwest end of the village. Offers Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner.

            Hermit's Rest

            Located 7 miles/11 km west of Grand Canyon Village at the end of Hermit Road; open daily, year-round.

            • Hermit's Rest Snack Bar - Located at Hermit's Rest. Offers quick snacks all day.

            Desert View

            The East Entrance to the park at Desert View is open 24 hours/day, 7 days/week.
            Desert View Drive is a 23 mile/ 37 km scenic road between Desert View and Grand Canyon Village, and is open to all vehicles.

            • Trading Post - Coffee and Ice Cream
            • Desert View Market and Deli
            • Desert View Gas Station
              Pay-at-the-Pump Fuel: 24 hours
             
             

            South Rim (Outside of the Park)

            Groceries, restaurants and supplies are available in the gateway community of Tusayan 7 miles/11 km south of Grand Canyon Village.

            • Tusayan Chamber of Commerce has detailed information on their website


            During the summer months, every 45 minutes, the free Tusayan (Purple) Route shuttle bus connects Tusayan with Grand Canyon Visitor Center in the park.

            North Rim Restaurants and Groceries (Inside the Park)

            The North Rim of Grand Canyon National Park has reopened for the 2026 season —including Highway 67, the Cape Royal Road, Point Imperial Road, and the North Kaibab Trail. Visit North Rim - Current Status and Closures for more detailed information and the latest updates.

            • NO WATER is available on the North Rim during the 2026 season. Plan ahead and ensure you have necessary provisions before visiting the North Rim.
            • North Rim General Store (located adjacent to the North Rim Campground)— dry goods, grab-and-go food, single serve water bottles and gallons of water available for purchase, open daily from 9 a.m. - 4 p.m.
            • Gas Station — CLOSED for the season; gas is available at Jacob Lake and the North Rim Country Store, located outside of the park.

            North Rim (Outside of the Park)

            • Kaibab Lodge Restaurant - Seasonal - 928-638-2389 - 18 miles/29 km north
            • North Rim Country Store - Seasonal - 928-638-2383 - 18 miles/29 km north
            • Jacob Lake Inn - Open all year. 928-643-7232 - 45 miles/72 km north

            What You Can Do

            Check out the links below for suggestions on simple things you can do to help protect the park and show that you, too, think "Green is Grand!"

            Refill your water bottles!

            Did you know that you can reduce plastics in the waste stream, litter along trails and walkways and greenhouse gas emissions just by refilling a reusable water bottle? Go to Grand Canyon's Reduce, Reuse, Refill page to learn more.

            Travel Green!

            It's a great way to help the Grand Canyon and the environment. Go to the Traveling Green page for helpful tips on planning your next green adventure.
