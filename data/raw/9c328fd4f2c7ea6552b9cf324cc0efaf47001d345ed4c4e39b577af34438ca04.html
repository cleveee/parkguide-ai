            Car and RV Campgrounds

            NOTE: Grand Canyon National Park remains in stage 1 fire restrictions year-round which requires that a campfire always be in a designated fire ring within a maintained campground. Inner canyon backcountry users can use gas cook stoves, but campfires and other open fires are never allowed below the canyon rim.

            • Mather Campground (No Hook-ups) OPEN ALL YEAR Grand Canyon Village.
              The Mather Campground office kiosk - at the entrance to the campground is open. Reservations are recommended between March 1, and November 30, 2026 and may be made up to 6 months in advance. There are 15, first come, first served campsites that go on sale at the campground office at 8 am each day. Details. Due to Stage 4 water restrictions, Mather Campground is dry camping only until further notice. All outdoor wood fires are prohibited.

            • Trailer Village RV Campground Due to Stage 4 water restrictions, Trailer Village is closed until further notice.

            • Camper Services (coin-operated laundry and showers) at the entrance to Mather Campground. Due to Stage 4 water restrictions, Camper Services is closed until further notice.

            • Desert View Campground (No Hook-ups) OPEN FOR THE SEASON
              Due to high demand, Desert View Campground now requires advanced reservations. Book reservations up to 6 months in advance for the 2026 season (Saturday, April 11, through Sunday, October 18, 2026). Details Due to Stage 4 water restrictions, Desert View Campground is dry camping only until further notice. Outdoor wood fires are prohibited.

            • North Rim Campground (No Hook-ups, No Water) OPEN FOR THE SEASON
              The North Rim Campground is open through Oct. 15, 2026. Book reservations in advance. Details

            • Kaibab Rim (Orange) Route Schedule >
              First bus is at 6 am—Last bus is one hour after sunset.
            • Provides transportation from the Visitor Center Shuttle Bus Terminal to South Kaibab Trailhead, Yaki Point, Pipe Creek Overlook, Mather Point and Yavapai Geology Museum.

            Need access to or from the South Kaibab Trailhead when the shuttle buses are not running? Contact Xanterra Taxi Service at (928) 638-2631.

            • Hikers' Express Shuttle Schedule >
              Early morning bus between the village and South Kaibab Trailhead.
              • Departs Bright Angel Lodge at 6 am, 7 am, and 8 am.
              • Backcountry Information Center at 6:10 am, 7:10 am and 8:10 am.
              • Visitor Center Shuttle Bus Terminal at 6:20 am, 7:20 am and 8:20 am.

            • Hermits Rest (Red) Route Schedule >
              First bus is at 8 am—last bus is one hour after sunset.
              Starts in the village Historic District, then travels along a 7 mile (11 km) scenic road with outstanding views of Grand Canyon. Round trip takes 80 minutes (without getting off the bus).

            Desert View is Open

            Desert View is a small settlement on the South Rim located 23 miles/ 37 km east of Grand Canyon Village, and near the eastern edge of Grand Canyon. Arizona State Route 64, also known as Desert View Drive, is a 23 mile (37 km) scenic road that connects Desert View with Grand Canyon Village. Desert View Drive is open to all vehicles throughout the year. (Unless temporarily closed by snow)

            • The East Entrance to the park at Desert View is open 24/7.

            North Rim

            North Rim - Current Status and Closures
            The North Rim of Grand Canyon National Park is open for the 2026 season. The North Kaibab Trail closed on August 29, due to a flash flood. Visit the link above for more detailed information on North Rim status and conditions.

            • NO WATER is available on the North Rim during the 2026 season. Plan ahead and ensure you have necessary provisions before visiting the North Rim.
            • Cape Royal Road and Point Imperial Road will be temporarily closed from sunset Oct. 15 through sunrise Nov. 3, 2026, to accommodate critical road repair and rehabilitation work.
            • North Rim General Store (located adjacent to the North Rim Campground)—dry goods, grab-and-go food, single serve water bottles and gallons of water available for purchase, open daily from 9 am to 4 pm. Will close for the season on Oct. 15 at 4 p.m.
            • Gas Station—CLOSED for the season; gas is available at Jacob Lake and the North Rim Country Store, located outside of the park.
            • North Rim Backcountry Information Center, located adjacent to the North Rim Campground—Staffed daily from 8 am to 12 pm. Will close for the season on Oct. 15 at 12 p.m.
