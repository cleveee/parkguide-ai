          South Entrance Station

          Use this webcam to help time your South Rim arrival and to check winter road conditions. During spring break, summer, and fall weekends, there can be long lines with up to two hour wait times between 10 am and 4 pm. This view refreshes every minute. The entrance station is about 1.6 miles (2.5 km) north of the gateway town of Tusayan, Arizona, and 4.9 miles (7.8 km) south of Grand Canyon Visitor Center, Check that the time and date are current (upper left in the photo).

          Park Road Conditions and Closures

           

          South Rim Roads

          Note: unexpected road closures and reopenings also appear in an alert at the top of this webpage.

          • Driving Directions to the South Rim Grand Canyon National Park.

          (Unlike the North Rim, the South Rim of the park remains open all year)

          • Traveling? Call 928-638-7496 for current park road closures/reopenings, or follow Grand Canyon National Park on X (Twitter) for updates.

          During winter storms, travelers should expect winter driving conditions and some roadways may be temporarily closed while snow is cleared. Be prepared for slippery, snow covered roads and limited visibility. Drive with care. During the winter, road conditions can change rapidly.

          The following road status information is valid from Mond          ay, March 2, 2026 at 1:15 pm, and it will remain valid until conditions change. At that time the information below will be updated.

          South Entrance Road OPEN

          • South Entrance Road is open to Grand Canyon Village.

          Desert View Drive (SR 64) OPEN

          • Desert View Drive is usually open to all vehicles throughout the year, unless temporarily closed by snow and ice.
          • The distance from Grand Canyon Village to Desert View and the East Entrance to the park is 23 miles (37 km).

          Please drive with caution and at speeds appropriate to the road conditions. Watch for deer and elk, and coyotes in the roadway.

          .

          Hermit Road (West Rim Drive) OPEN

          Hermit Road is only open to vehicles under 22 feet -total length
          • Hermit Road is open to private vehicles during the months of December, January and February - unless closed by snow.
          • During the busy season (March 1, through November 30), Hermit Road is closed to private vehicles. Motorized access is by the free Hermit Road (Red) Route shuttle bus or by Commercial Motorcoach Tour

          During summer thunderstorms, or other inclement weather, shuttle bus service will be suspended on Hermit Road. When service is suspended due to inclement weather, shuttle buses with additional capacity will stop anywhere along the Hermit Road it is safe to do so, and pick up visitors.

           

          North Rim Roads OPEN

          North Rim - Current Status and Closures

          Select areas of the North Rim have reopened. Follow the link above for the most recent updates.

          Visitors should plan ahead and be prepared for a more remote experience this year, with limited services available as the park continues recovery and restoration work.

          The 2026 season will focus mainly on day use and limited backcountry access, with some scenic drives, viewpoints, and trails open to visitors. Please keep in mind that potable water will not be available on the North Rim, so be sure to bring plenty of water and food for your visit.


          Scenic roads to Cape Royal and Point Imperial: OPEN
          *Note:           Cape Royal Road and Point Imperial Road will be temporarily closed from sunset Oct. 15 through sunrise Nov. 3, 2026, to accommodate critical road repair and rehabilitation work.
          NOTE: RVs over 22 feet long, and vehicle-trailer combinations with a total length over 22 feet, are not permitted on Cape Royal Road and the Scenic Drive due to narrow, windy roads with blind corners

          • To check the latest road closure/reopening information, please call our NPS Roads Line at 928-638-7496
