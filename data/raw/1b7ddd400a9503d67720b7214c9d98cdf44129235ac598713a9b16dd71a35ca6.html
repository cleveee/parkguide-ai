          Lodging

          Overnight guest lodging closure in effect

          • Effective Monday, August 31, 2026, all park concessions will halt overnight accommodations. This includes hotel facilities operated by Xanterra, such as El Tovar, Bright Angel Lodge, Maswik Lodge, and Phantom Ranch, as well as Delaware North’s Yavapai Lodge and Trailer Village. Overnight hotel accommodations located outside the park in the town of Tusayan will not be impacted by changes in park operations.  
          • This action was taken because a flash flood on August 29, caused extensive damage to infrastructure in the inner canyon, including the Transcanyon Waterline, which supplies water from the canyon for use in the park. The waterline is currently not operational, leaving the park with limited water reserves and requiring significant water conservation measures to ensure available supplies can be maintained. News Release - Aug. 30, 2026.

            Phantom Ranch

            Visit our Key Hiking Messages webpage for current trail closures near Phantom Ranch.

            CLOSED — As of August 29, 2026, due to flash flooding:
            The entirety of Phantom Ranch, including Bright Angel Campground, the Phantom Ranch Canteen, and Phantom Ranch cabins. The Black Bridge and Silver Bridge spanning the Colorado River.

            The entirety of the North Kaibab Trail. This includes the North Kaibab Trailhead to Phantom Ranch. This closure is a result of significant flash flooding to the Bright Angel Canyon on 8/29.

            There is no estimated reopening of these closure areas at this time.

            Phantom Ranch, at the bottom of the Grand Canyon, is a popular destination for both hikers and mule riders. Overnight dormitories and cabins can be reserved and meals are available for purchase. Advance reservations for meals and lodging are required.

            Phantom Ranch Reservations are made through Grand Canyon National Park Lodges (Xanterra Parks and Resorts) via an on-line lottery 15 months in advance.

            (An NPS backcountry camping permit is not required for overnight stays in Phantom Ranch accomodations.) The park's Backcountry Information Center does not make reservations for Phantom Ranch lodging or meals. Reservations are made through Grand Canyon National Park Lodges.

            Reservations for the nearby Bright Angel Campground (NPS) are made through the park's Backcountry Information Center.
